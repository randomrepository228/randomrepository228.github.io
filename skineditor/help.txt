1 - Go down a layer
2 - Go up a layer
W or Up - Move layer up
A or Left - Move layer left
S or Down - Move layer down
D or Right - Move layer right
E - Rotate layer 45 degrees clockwise
Q - Rotate layer 45 degrees counterclockwise
N - Open new layer dialog
R - Flip layer on X axis
T - Flip layer on Y axis
- - Shrink Layer
+ - Grow Layer
F - Pick layer color
G - Change layer shape
Ctrl+X - Cut
Ctrl+C - Copy
Ctrl+V - Paste
Alt+Up or Alt+W - Move layer up (in editor)
Alt+Down or Alt+S - Move layer down (in editor)
Alt+D or Del - Delete layer
